Ticket 300: Pot C rebuilt both ways, C 01 measured, and its gap located 36 mm off the line that seats it

The conservator asked to see the Pot C reassembly with his own eye, ours beside the
authors', and to know whether C 01's interior-surface scan gap cost it a seat. Pot C
scored 4/4 sherds and 5/5 joins in both arms of ticket 298, but a pot-level score does
not certify a single piece, and the assembler reads Breakline_0 - the boundary of the
interior surface, the one surface carrying the gap.

Staging needed the geometry, and 298 stored only transforms, so both reassemblies were
rebuilt from the corpus meshes and the stored T_0to_0..T_3to_3. The convention was not
assumed: p_world = R p_local + t was tested against the authors' own Ground Truth
transforms, whose five joins close at 0.045-0.102 mm, with the pair the GT graph says
does NOT join (C03-C04) as the control at 12.105 mm; the inverted reading scatters every
piece hundreds of millimetres. Nothing inverted, nothing fixed. Both arms' stored
transforms reproduce the same seams (authb 0.020-0.066 mm, oursd 0.035-0.100 mm), so the
rebuilt geometry is the geometry that was scored.

Measured answer: the gap did not cost a seat. C 01 is the only non-watertight piece of the
four (C 02-C 04 have zero boundary edges); its four open rims include a 101.97 mm-perimeter
hole lying 0.29 mm on our interior Surface_0 and, 11.35 mm away across the wall, a
116.82 mm rim - the scan gap, on the flat of the sherd. Our Bl0 line (207 dots) is 36.1 mm
from the nearest rim and the authors' Breakline_0 (211 dots) 36.2 mm, so the line the
assembler reads runs the real break edge, not the gap. C 01's joins close to
0.035-0.100 mm and its relative pose error is 0.438-0.634 deg / 2.548-4.351 mm (oursd) and
0.333-0.862 / 3.004-3.886 (authb) - inside the assembler's 20.05 deg / 50 mm tolerance and
the same order as its neighbours', so it is not the outlier of the hand in either arm.

Weight kept honest: the per-piece 152-153.7 deg "frame-free" rotation is the frame
difference and is recorded as such; the sub-millimetre per-piece residuals after one
global alignment are the pose error. One hand, four pieces, one seating each - this settles
C 01 for these two arms and says nothing about Pot C's other released pieces or Pot J 01.

Staged as visual-qa pairs pota300_potC_arms (authors beside ours, same four pieces, same
colours, GT vessel frame, z up) and pota300_potC_c01 (C 01 seated with its rims marked in
magenta and both interior lines drawn). stage_pair.py reports 0.10 mm median surface
coincidence between the two builds, under the 5.0 mm same-frame gate; check_annotations
exits 0 and every check_stage_pair assertion passes (its exit 1 is a Windows temp-dir
teardown bug on its own fixtures). Geometry stays in artifacts/pota300 and
artifacts/visual-qa (both gitignored); no algorithm, threshold or assembler was touched and
the campaign was not re-run. Status left at in-progress for the lead.
